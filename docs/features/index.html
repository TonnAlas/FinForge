Features

A plain-English tour of what FinForge can do.

FeatureDescription
Live Market DataPrices and company fundamentals refresh automatically from the market.
Fast and ResponsiveData is stored in an optimized format (Parquet) so the app stays quick as your library grows.
Desktop WorkspaceA modern desktop app with simple sidebar navigation keeps everything within reach.
Excel DashboardAn interactive dashboard built directly into Excel, so it feels familiar.
Interactive ChartsZoomable charts compare multiple stocks and ratios side by side, with dual axes and range sliders.
Custom RatiosBuild your own financial ratios with a visual builder, with no manual formula typing.
Multi-Stock TrackingFollow and compare many companies at the same time.
Ranking DashboardScore a set of stocks across user-selected metrics with a weighted multi-criteria model, shape each metric's scoring curve visually with draggable handles, save presets, and explore a full-screen sortable results table.
Automatic CalculationsRatios update on their own whenever new data arrives.
Statement ImportPick which line items to pull into Excel, from the balance sheet, income statement, or cash flow.
Research SearchSearch for equity research and analyst notes from inside the app.
Company ProfilesA clean summary of key information for any company.
Cash Flow ImportBring cash flow statements in alongside the balance sheet and income statement.
Template SystemSave and reload workbook layouts so your setup is ready next time.
Excel Add-inLaunch FinForge directly from the Excel ribbon.
