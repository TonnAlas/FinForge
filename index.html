For those who respect fundamentals

FinForge

Turn raw market data into clear answers. FinForge brings live stock data, side-by-side charts, and a familiar Excel dashboard together in one simple app, so you can research companies and track your own financial ratios without writing a line of code.

Get startedSee features

Features

Live Market Data

Up-to-date prices and company fundamentals, pulled in automatically.

Fast and Responsive

Data is stored efficiently so the app stays quick, even with a large library.

Clean Desktop App

A modern workspace with search, charts, and tools in one simple sidebar.

Works in Excel

An interactive dashboard built right into the spreadsheet you already know.

Interactive Charts

Zoomable charts that let you compare stocks and ratios side by side.

Build Your Own Formulas

Create custom financial ratios visually, with no coding or formula typing.

Track Multiple Stocks

Follow and compare as many companies as you want, all in one place.

Ranking Dashboard

Score and rank companies against your own weighted metrics, with adjustable curves and a sortable results view.

Pick What You Import

Choose exactly which line items appear in your workbook.

Find Research Fast

Search for analyst and equity research without leaving the app.

Company Snapshots

See the key details for any company you follow, at a glance.

Save Your Setup

Reuse saved workbook templates so you never start from scratch.

One Click from Excel

Launch the full workspace straight from the Excel ribbon.

Getting started

  1. Download the project.
  2. Run setup.bat.
  3. Launch launch_finforge.bat and open your dashboard.
